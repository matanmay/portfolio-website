PhD Student & HCI Researcher

Hello! I'm Matan Mayerowicz, a PhD student in Information Systems at the University of Haifa, researching human-computer interaction and human-LLM interaction. I hold an M.Sc. in Information Systems (GPA 95, Dean's List, Cum Laude) with an HCI specialization and a B.Sc. in Information Systems (Cum Laude). I build research systems and user-facing interfaces with Python, ReactJS, C#, Java, and SQL, and combine that with data analysis, teaching, and team leadership experience. I am passionate about developing efficient, scalable solutions that are technically sound and impactful.
